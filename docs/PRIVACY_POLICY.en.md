# Privacy policy — Cadryl

Preparation version: 30 September 2026.

Declared publisher: Unicorn Who Dev, France. Contact: unicornwhodev@gmail.com.
The complete legal identity and business address must be provided before commercial distribution.

## Your projects

Cadryl stores projects, images copied for processing, annotations, indexes,
history, chosen models and verification receipts on your device. The inference
and training functions provided in the application run locally. There is no
Cadryl account or audience analytics integration.

You choose accessible documents through Android's document picker. Granting
file access is not advertising consent. Notifications track background work.
Automatic Android backups of the application are disabled. A dataset export
does not back up an entire project.

## Connections and recipients

Downloads contact Hugging Face or the HTTPS host you choose. These services
receive your IP address and the request needed for the download. A supplied
HF token is encrypted with Android Keystore to access authorized resources;
it is not included in exported packs.

HF publication sends the batch's images, annotations and metadata to the
confirmed destination. Visibility depends on repository settings. Check rights,
personal data and the recipient's terms before publication. SAF copies go to
the document provider you select, which may be a cloud service. Local purging
does not delete external copies.

## Retention and control

Local data stays until you explicitly delete it. Purging a batch preserves human
annotations, duplicate prevention history and receipts, and requires a copy
that has been read back. Deleting an entire project requires a separate
confirmation. Uninstalling removes private application storage, but not source
files or external copies. Keep your backups before uninstalling.

You can revoke Android access and remove your HF token. For data held by an
external service, use that service's controls too. For access, correction or
deletion requests concerning data held by the publisher, contact
unicornwhodev@gmail.com. You can complain to the French data protection authority,
CNIL: https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes.

## Public edition

This edition contains no ads, subscription or billing SDK. It requires no
AI Studio key, Cadryl account or Cadryl backend. HF connections, model downloads
and remote SAF providers are optional and depend on your choices.
